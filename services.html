FAQ

Questions, answered

Is NexFlow suitable for very small teams?

Yes. The Starter plan is built for solo founders and teams of up to three people, and you can upgrade whenever you need more.

Do I need technical skills to set it up?

No. You connect your tools with a few clicks, choose ready-made workflows, or describe what you want to the AI assistant.

How does NexFlow keep my data safe?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, each workspace is isolated, and you can enable single sign-on and audit logs.

Can I manage several businesses or branches?

Yes. Multi-tenant workspaces let you run separate brands, branches or client accounts from one login, each with its own team and permissions.

What payment methods can my customers use?

Customers can pay invoices by card or bank transfer through supported payment providers such as Paystack and Stripe.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. You can change or cancel your plan whenever you like.
