About NexFlow

We help small businesses work like big ones

NexFlow AI gives growing teams the automation, insight and billing tools that used to be reserved for enterprises, in one simple platform.

Our story

Too many tools, not enough time

Small business owners spend hours every week copying data between spreadsheets, chasing payments and building reports by hand. The software that could help is usually built for large companies, priced like it, and takes months to set up.

NexFlow was created to change that. We combined workflow automation, analytics and billing into one workspace, then added an AI assistant that understands your business, so anyone on the team can get answers and get work done.

Today NexFlow is built for retailers, logistics companies, agencies and service businesses that want to grow without adding more busywork.

  1. Step 1

    The problem

    SMEs juggle 8+ disconnected apps and lose hours to manual work every week.

  2. Step 2

    The idea

    One AI-powered workspace that connects sales, finance and operations.

  3. Step 3

    The platform

    Multi-tenant SaaS with automation, live analytics and built-in billing.

  4. Next

    The future

    Smarter recommendations that help every business plan its next move.
